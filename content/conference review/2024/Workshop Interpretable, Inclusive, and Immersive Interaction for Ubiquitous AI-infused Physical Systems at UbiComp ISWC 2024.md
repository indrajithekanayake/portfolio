UbiComp / ISWC is a premier interdisciplinary venue in which leading international researchers, designers, developers, and practitioners in the field present and discuss novel results in all aspects of ubiquitous, pervasive, and wearable computing. The conference will be held on 5-9th October 2024 in Melbourne, Australia.

I was invited as a reviewer by the Organizers of the Workshop on AI-infused Physical Systems (I4U) through ACM SIGCHI.
