ICIIS is a CORE C-ranked conference with an H-index of 18. There are only two engineering conferences in Sri Lanka with a CORE ranking, and ICIIS is one of them. I therefore consider ICIIS the best engineering research conference in Sri Lanka, and I’m glad to be part of the reviewing team.
